Hilo Negro Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Hilo Negro Studio ("the Studio") is a self-hosted tool used by the owner of the TikTok account Hilo Negro to produce, schedule and publish that account's own videos. This policy explains what data the Studio handles when a TikTok account is connected to it.

1. Data we receive from TikTok

TikTok permissionDataWhy we use it
user.info.basicOpen ID and display nameTo identify the connected account
user.info.statsFollower count, total likes, number of videosTo chart the account's growth
video.listThe account's public videos and their view, like, comment and share countsTo report which videos perform best
video.publishNo data is read; grants upload accessTo publish videos the owner has approved

We also store the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues, which are needed to make those requests. We never receive your TikTok password.

2. Data we do not collect

The Studio does not collect data about viewers, followers or commenters as individuals, and does not read private messages, contacts, drafts or private videos. It has no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics about visitors.

3. Where data is stored and who can see it

All data is stored on the computer or server where the account owner runs the Studio. Tokens are kept in a local file that is not shared or published. We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with anyone. Data is not used for advertising or to build profiles of people.

4. Other services

To produce videos, the Studio may send story text (not TikTok data) to an AI text provider (Anthropic) and a text-to-speech provider (for example ElevenLabs), and it reads public posts from Reddit through Reddit's official API. Those services process that text under their own privacy policies.

5. How long we keep data

Statistics are kept while the account stays connected, so that reports can show trends. Tokens are kept until they expire or the account is disconnected.

6. Your choices

  • Disconnect: revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions. The Studio can no longer read or publish anything.
  • Delete: ask us through the support page below and we will delete the stored tokens and all statistics for the account within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access or correct your data, or to complain to your data protection authority.

7. Children

The Studio is not directed to children and is not used by anyone under 18.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

9. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests: open a request on our support page
